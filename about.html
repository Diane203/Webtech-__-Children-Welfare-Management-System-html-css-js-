ABOUT HOPE HAVEN

Caring for Rwanda's Children

Hope Haven is a comprehensive children welfare management system designed to help orphanages and child care centers manage the welfare, care, and development of orphaned and vulnerable children under 18, with special focus on those with disabilities.

OUR MISSION

To provide a safe, loving, and nurturing environment for every child, ensuring their physical, emotional, and educational needs are met. We empower vulnerable children to become confident, capable, and compassionate members of society.

OUR VISION

A Rwanda where every child has access to quality care, education, and the opportunity to reach their full potential — regardless of their background, ability, or circumstances.

What We Do

🏠

Residential Care

We provide a safe, family-like home for orphaned and vulnerable children, meeting all their daily needs.

🎓

Education Support

Every child is enrolled in school. We provide uniforms, materials, tutoring, and monitor academic progress.

🏥

Healthcare & Disability Support

Regular medical checkups, specialized treatment, physiotherapy, hearing aids, and vision care.

💼

Life Skills & Transition

Preparing older children for independent living through vocational training and mentorship.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Hope Haven's mission? +

Hope Haven is dedicated to providing comprehensive welfare management for orphaned and vulnerable children, with specialized care for those with disabilities. We ensure every child has access to safety, education, healthcare, and the opportunity to reach their full potential.

How long has Hope Haven been operating? +

Hope Haven was founded in 2018 in Kigali, Rwanda. Since then, we have grown from caring for a handful of children to now supporting 24+ children with a dedicated team of 12 staff members and 8 active sponsors.

What makes Hope Haven different? +

We focus on holistic care — not just food and shelter, but also education, healthcare, emotional support, and life skills. Our specialized disability program provides physiotherapy, hearing aids, vision care, and other assistive devices that many centers cannot offer.

How can I support Hope Haven? +

You can support us by sponsoring a child, making a one-time donation, volunteering your time, or donating supplies like clothing, school materials, or medical equipment. Visit our Contact page to get started.

Can I visit the children? +

Yes! We welcome scheduled visits from supporters, sponsors, and volunteers. Please contact us at least one week in advance so we can arrange a visit that respects the children's routines and privacy.

Are donations tax-deductible? +

Yes, Hope Haven is a registered non-profit organization in Rwanda. All donations are eligible for tax deduction in accordance with Rwandan law. We provide official receipts for all contributions above 50,000 FRw.

How do I know my sponsorship is going to the right place? +

We provide quarterly impact reports to all sponsors showing how their funds are used. Sponsors can also visit the child they sponsor, receive regular updates and photos, and communicate with our staff through our secure system.
