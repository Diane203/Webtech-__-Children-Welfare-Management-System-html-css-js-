Frequently Asked Questions

How can I sponsor a child?+

Visit our Sponsor page to choose a child and complete the process online. Monthly sponsorship starts at 25,000 FRw and covers education, healthcare, and daily needs.

Can I visit the children?+

Yes! We welcome scheduled visits. Please contact us at least one week in advance to arrange a visit that respects the children's routines and privacy.

How are donations used?+

All donations go directly to child welfare: 60% education, 25% healthcare, 10% daily needs, and 5% administrative costs. We publish annual reports for full transparency.

Do you accept in-kind donations?+

Absolutely! We accept clothing, school supplies, bedding, food, and medical supplies. Please contact us first to confirm current needs.
